# Sangat packaging specification (v2)

Artwork: `packaging-v2/` (built by `tools/packaging_v2.py`). Mockups: `mockups-v2/`.

| | 1 kg | 5 kg |
|---|---|---|
| Format (assumed) | Stand-up pouch with zip, 180 × 280 mm, bottom gusset 2 × 45 mm | Side-gusset bag with handle, 300 × 450 mm, sides 100 mm |
| Keep-out zones | Top seal 12 mm, zip at 30 mm: no content above 34 mm except the tab | Top seal 15 mm, handle die-cut 80 × 22 mm at 22 mm: no content above 52 mm except the tab |
| Front | Tab + symbol, wordmark, tagline (EN + HI), PREMIUM / BASMATI RICE, NATURALLY AROMATIC · THOUGHTFULLY GROWN, partnership line, landscape, sweep, gold ears, (V1) rice window, veg mark, net quantity | Same, scaled |
| Back | Symbol, "A shared journey / *for a brighter tomorrow*", brand copy, four value icons, farmer illustration, nutrition + legal panel, barcode, FSSAI logo, **QR strip** | Same |
| Sides / gusset | Gusset: Forest with a row of symbols and the tagline | "Good food grows from shared thinking." + symbol + tagline + landscape |

## Variations

- **V1 Ivory Landscape**: ivory ground, forest tab, day landscape, clear rice window. The everyday range.
- **V2 Forest Edition**: forest ground, gold tab, dusk landscape, no window. Gift and festive packs, or the 5 kg.

## Finishes (intent, to confirm on press proofs)

Matte paper-touch laminate on ivory; hot-foil gold for the ear, rules, ears and window edge; clear window film
(V1); embossed wordmark on the 5 kg front and blind-embossed symbol on the tab; deeper spot matte on forest areas.

## Placeholders (must be completed before print)

Converter dielines; ingredients; nutrition per 100 g; cooking guide; MRP; batch, packed-on and best-before;
packer/marketer name and address; FSSAI licence number and logo; official veg-mark artwork; customer care;
EAN-13 (GS1 India); the QR code and short URL. Regulatory review (FSSAI Labelling and Display Regulations) of
type sizes and panel placement. "Naturally aromatic" and "thoughtfully grown" are descriptive lines to be
confirmed by MVS; no certification, origin or impact claim is printed on the pack.
