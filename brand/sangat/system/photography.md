# Sangat photography direction (v2)

**No photographs exist yet.** The board's photographic look (warm table light, ceramic bowl of cooked rice, soft
leaf shadows) is the art direction for the shoot; until then the website keeps its 3D grain and the packs use the
drawn landscape. Never use stock photos of "farmers" to stand in for the families: the people must be real and
consenting.

- **Light:** warm, low, late-afternoon; soft shadows, a hint of leaf shadow; no flash, no HDR.
- **Table:** worn wood, linen, stone or brass; a matte ceramic bowl; the long grain visible; a single herb sprig at most.
- **Packs:** shot on the same table, front three-quarter; the window must show real rice.
- **Field:** real families and the MVS team, with consent, at work; captions only with confirmed names and places.
- **Colour:** warm neutrals; greens pulled towards Forest and Sage; highlights towards Ivory; never saturated.
- **Never:** staged smiles at camera, children as props, flying grains, glossy studio reflections, any image that
  implies a certification, a place or a person MVS has not confirmed.
