# Sangat photography direction

**No photographs exist yet.** These are art-direction rules for the shoot; until then the website keeps its 3D grain
and drawn illustration, and packs use no imagery. Never use stock photos of "farmers" to stand in for the real
families: the brand's credibility depends on the people being real and consenting.

## Three subjects, one light

1. **People, together.** Participating farming families and the MVS team, photographed with consent, at work and in
   conversation: hands, shared tasks, groups of two or three. Eye-level, unposed, names and places captioned only
   when confirmed.
2. **The field and the grain.** Close, quiet details: water standing in a levelled plot, a panicle against the sky,
   grain in the hand at harvest. Shallow depth of field; one subject per frame.
3. **The table.** Cooked basmati served simply, in daylight, on matte ceramic or brass; the long grain visible.
   Never a styled "feast" spread.

## Treatment

- Light: natural, low-angle morning or late-afternoon sun; soft shadows. No flash, no HDR.
- Colour: warm neutrals; greens pulled towards Sangat Forest and Paddy Sage; highlights towards Basmati Ivory.
  Never saturate to "organic green".
- Composition: generous negative space for the hanging line and type; subjects off-centre.
- Duotone (for low-quality source images and the QR pages): Night Forest shadows to Basmati Ivory highlights.

## Never

Stock farmers, staged smiles at camera, children as props, sunbursts, flying grains, glossy studio packshots with
reflections, any image that implies a certification, a place or a person that MVS has not confirmed.
