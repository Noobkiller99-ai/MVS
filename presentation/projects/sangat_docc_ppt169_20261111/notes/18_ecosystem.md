Act five translates the work into a development pathway. What a shopper sees as one pack is really a chain of eight hand-offs: farmers, farmer groups or arthiyas, processors and millers, a quality lead or laboratory, a packaging vendor, a logistics partner, retailers and online channels, and finally consumers. Each one needs clear guidance, written specifications, lot identification and reconciliation. We see MVS's role as the layer above the chain, responsible for coordination, evidence governance, brand oversight, claim approvals and grievance escalation. This is a proposed operating framework, not one that is running today.
