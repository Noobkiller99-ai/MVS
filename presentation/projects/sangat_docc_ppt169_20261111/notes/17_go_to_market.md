Our go-to-market logic is to prove the rice first and then build trust in the story. For product, that means a written specification covering grain length, ageing, moisture, broken grain and purity, with batch controls and cooking guidance. On price, current listings suggest roughly 180 to 220 rupees per kilogram, and we recommend matching competitors on quality and price while rechecking before fixing anything. For place, the pilot uses premium grocery, modern trade, online channels where feasible, direct ordering and sampling through MVS's networks. For promotion, we propose cooking demonstrations, blind sampling, retailer education, recipe content and the QR story.
