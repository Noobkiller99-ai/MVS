Act one is about entering the development ecosystem. MVS was founded in 1996 as a non-profit and works across Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, Haryana, Uttarakhand and Meghalaya, on women's empowerment, sustainable agriculture, water conservation, climate resilience, education and rural development. It works with farmers, rural households and self-help groups. Through PRERANA it supports farm and non-farm livelihoods for rural women and strengthens women-led institutions, and the PRANA and PRERANA field and programme teams facilitated our interactions. Our understanding evolved in three steps: from an NGO profile, to its programmes, to its people. The relationships those field teams have built turned out to be the real foundation any brand would need.
