# Photo insertion guide — Sangat DoCC final presentation

The deck already uses all 15 field photographs uploaded to the repository. They are placed in the chronology of the story, not in a gallery. Eight activities still have no photograph. Each of those has a designed placeholder picture: a tinted frame with corner crop marks, the rice-ear mark and a small label such as `INSERT PICKLE-MAKING INTERACTION PHOTO`. Captions sit outside the frames as separate, editable text.

## How to replace a placeholder (PowerPoint)

1. Open **Home → Arrange → Selection Pane**. Every picture is named, for example `phx-s04-pickles`. Placeholders start with `phx-`; real photos start with `ph`.
2. Click the placeholder, then right-click → **Change Picture → From a File…** and pick your photo. PowerPoint keeps the frame's position and size.
3. If the subject is off-centre, use **Picture Format → Crop → Fill** and drag the photo inside the frame.
4. Edit the caption beneath the frame if you want more detail, such as a name or date.

For a clean replacement, use photos at least 1200 px on the long side, in the orientation listed below.
