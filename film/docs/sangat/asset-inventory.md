# Sangat launch reel: asset inventory

Every asset on screen or in the mix, where it came from, and what is still missing. Nothing in the film was
generated by an AI image or video model, and no person's likeness appears.

## Used

| Asset | File in the film project | Source | Licence / rights | How it is made |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| Cultivation (scene 3) | `public/sangat/footage/cultivation.mp4` | Same 3D scene, cultivation chapter | Own work | Shot `cultivation`, 165 frames |

## Missing, and what replaces it today

| Needed for the brief | Status | Placeholder in this cut | How to add it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authentic footage of the Manav Vikas Sansthan farmers (scene 3, "the people") | **Not supplied.** The brief says never to stage it, and no real person's likeness is generated. | Scene 3 shows the cultivation itself (plants in a flooded paddy) from the 3D scene, so the line "Grown through shared effort." stands on the work, not on invented people | Put the clip in `public/sangat/footage/` and render with `--props='{"farmerClip":"sangat/footage/<file>.mp4"}'`. It plays first in scene 3 and dissolves into the paddy from 10.3 s. Consent from everyone recognisable on screen is needed before release. |
| Licensed Indian instrumental (elegant, bansuri, sitar or santoor led) | **Not licensed.** No music library is connected to this session. | The original temporary score above, written to the same beat sheet | Licence a track (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Musicbed, or a commissioned piece), put it in `public/audio/` (gitignored, licensed audio never goes in the repo) and render with `--props='{"music":"audio/<file>.wav"}'`. Fit it with the `video-audio-stack` skill so the swell lands at 18 s and the resolution at 22 s. |
| Sound effects (grain ticks, paddy water, the pack set down) | Not sourced | None; the score carries a soft wind and water bed | Freesound (CC0 only) or Epidemic Sound SFX, placed with the `SfxCue` primitive in `src/lib/` |
| Real product photography of the printed packs | No printed packs yet | Artwork renders of the v3 fronts | Replace the two PNGs in `public/sangat/packs/` with cut-out photographs at the same aspect; the scene code does not change |
| 16:9 edition | Not requested by the brief | none | The scenes are written for 1080 x 1920; a landscape edition would need its own layout branches |

## External services and subscriptions

None was used, and none is required to render this cut.

| Service | What it would add | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Higgsfield (MCP) | AI cinematic B-roll (the marketing-videos workflow's default) | A Higgsfield account and its MCP server connected; the stills-first approval gate in `ai-cinematic-broll` applies. Not used, because the 3D scene already gives on-brand footage of the same grain and the brief forbids staged people |
| Epidemic Sound | Licensed music and SFX | A commercial subscription; files stay out of git |
| Freesound | CC0 sound effects | A free account to download |
| Remotion | The renderer | Free for individuals, non-profits and companies of up to three people; larger companies need a Remotion company licence (remotion.dev/license) |
