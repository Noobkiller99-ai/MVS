## 3. Brand Identity Rules & Preservations

1. **Logo & Wordmark Integrity**:
   - The Sangat wordmark contains custom-crafted letterforms, a distinctive swash `g`, and an embedded gold rice ear.
   - It must never be re-typeset, distorted, or modified.
2. **Packaging Authenticity**:
   - The packaging designs (`v1-ivory-landscape-1kg` and `v1-ivory-landscape-5kg`) represent the finalized brand look.
   - Packaging imagery in Scene 5 will use genuine mockups composited on warm ivory backgrounds with realistic shadows and soft specular highlights.
3. **Color Precision**:
   - Forest Green: `#0F3D2E`
   - Ivory Background: `#F9F6EE`
   - Sunehra Gold: `#C9A96B`
   - Earth Brown: `#8B6F47` (UI: `#7A5F3B`)
   - Sage Green: `#A7B89F`
   - Stone Neutral: `#D9D4C8`
   - Ink Black: `#13231C`
4. **Authenticity Guarantee**:
   - Real farmer photos from the Manav Vikas Sansthan (MVS) initiative are incorporated, ensuring dignity, respect, and zero stereotyping.
