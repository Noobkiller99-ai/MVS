## Detailed Scene-by-Scene Specifications

### SCENE 1: THE GRAIN (Frames 0 – 119 | 0.0s – 4.0s)
- **Objective:** Evoke awe and intimacy; introduce our protagonist — the individual basmati grain.
- **Cinematography:**
  - Camera Angle: Extreme macro eye-level, 100mm macro lens emulation with razor-thin depth of field ($f/2.8$).
  - Camera Movement: Very slow continuous orbital pan (15° arc) combined with subtle forward drift.
  - Lighting: Deep, warm earthy darkness (`#0A1A14`) punctured by a focused warm directional rim light (`#E2CB97`), catching the longitudinal ridges and crystalline translucency of the grain.
- **Graphic / Text Layer:**
  - `Frame 30 – 100`: Kinetic reveal in center-safe region.
  - Headline: *"Every grain has a beginning."*
  - Typography: Playfair Display Regular (Ivory `#F9F6EE`), with *"beginning."* in Playfair Italic highlighted in Gold Light (`#E2CB97`).
- **Sound Design:**
  - Sub-bass warm swell (`35Hz`) + delicate rustle of a single rice husk opening + distant warm cello drone.
- **Transition Out:**
  - At Frame 105, camera accelerates into the crystalline body of the grain; focal blur expands into warm golden morning mist.
