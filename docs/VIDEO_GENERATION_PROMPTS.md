# VIDEO GENERATION & CINEMATOGRAPHY PROMPTS: SANGAT LAUNCH FILM

**Target Model Architecture:** Google Veo 2 / Runway Gen-3 Alpha / Kling AI / High-Fidelity Diffusion  
**Master Format:** 9:16 Vertical (1080 × 1920) | 30 FPS  
**Color Science:** Rec.709, Master Grade: Sangat Brand Identity (Forest Green, Warm Ivory, Sunehra Gold, Earth Brown)  

---

## 1. Prompt Engineering Philosophy for Sangat

Every prompt is constructed with 13 mandatory cinematographic parameters:
1. `Subject`
2. `Environment`
3. `Composition`
4. `Camera Angle`
5. `Camera Movement`
6. `Lens & Sensor Characteristics`
7. `Lighting Scheme`
8. `Depth of Field`
9. `Pacing & Motion Rate`
10. `Color Palette Tokens`
11. `Emotional Tone`
12. `Duration & Frame Count`
13. `Transition Boundary`
